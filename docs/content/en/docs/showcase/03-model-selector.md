# Avatars & Model Selector

![Avatars & Model Selector](/showcase/hero-03-model-selector.avif)

The **Model Selector & Avatar Studio** provides a high-performance management environment for 3D and 2D avatars. Supporting VRM 0.x/1.0, Live2D Cubism (v2.1/v3/v4), Spine 2D, and MMD formats, the selector combines multi-column browsing, an interactive 3D coverflow carousel, cloud catalog downloads, and the powerful Model Customizer for real-time expression, VFX, and texture authoring.

---

## High-Density Browsing & Coverflow

![Dense Model Grid](/showcase/model-selector-grid.avif)

- **Dense 6-Column & Classic Layouts**: Switch between high-density thumbnail grids for large libraries and focused 2-column detail views.
- **Interactive 3D Coverflow**: Real-time rendering carousel allowing creators to scrub through avatars with live idle motion settling.
- **Full-Text Tag & Group Search**: Filter models instantly by renderer type, series tag, artist attribution, or custom user tags.

![3D Coverflow Carousel](/showcase/model-selector-coverflow.avif)

---

## Cloud Catalog & 1-Click Downloads

![Cloud Catalog Browser](/showcase/model-selector-cloud-catalog.avif)

The integrated **Cloud Catalog** gives access to a curated repository of pre-configured, stage-ready avatars:
- **Over 90+ Avatars**: Browse VRM, Live2D, and Spine models with transparent licensing and preview clips.
- **Background Downloads**: Single-click downloads with background progress tracking, auto-extraction, and instant model catalog registration.

---

## Model Customizer & Embodiment Studio

The embedded Model Customizer provides deep authoring capabilities for avatar rigging, visual effects, and expressions:

### AI Expression Curation & Spine 2D Runtimes

![AI Expression Curation](/showcase/model-customizer-ai-curation.avif)

- **AI Expression Curation**: Automatically analyzes blendshapes and parameters to suggest optimal facial expressions for AIRI's 10 canonical emotion keys.
- **Spine 2D Skeletal Animation**: Native runtime support for Spine 2D models, including dynamic skins, physics attachments, and event callbacks.

![Spine 2D Skeletal Runtime](/showcase/model-customizer-motions-spine.avif)

### Procedural VRM VFX & Auras

![Procedural VRM VFX & Auras](/showcase/model-customizer-vfx-auras.avif)

Injects procedural visual flair onto avatar stages:
- **Magical Auras & Particle Systems**: Dynamic glowing rings, particle emitters, and floating halos reactive to avatar speech and emotions.
- **Custom Shader Stacks**: Live tuning of MToon rim lighting, shading shifts, and emission colors.

### V-HACK: Real-Time Texture Surgery

![V-HACK Texture Surgery Deck](/showcase/model-customizer-vhack-texture-deck.avif)

The **V-HACK / God MODE** deck enables live avatar modification without external 3D software:
- **Live Mesh Surgery**: Inspect avatar hierarchy trees, toggle submeshes, and adjust material blending on the fly.
- **Texture Forge**: In-memory texture replacement and AI-driven texture restyling with instantaneous stage preview and lossless GLB export.

## Key Capabilities

- **Universal Multi-Format Support**: VRM 0.x/1.0, Live2D Cubism, Spine 2.x/3.x/4.x, and MMD.
- **Dual Display Modes**: 6-column high-density browser and 3D coverflow carousel.
- **Curated Cloud Catalog**: 90+ stage-ready avatars with 1-click background downloading.
- **AI Blendshape Curation**: Automatic emotion-to-morph target mapping.
- **Procedural Stage VFX**: Dynamic particle systems, magical auras, and rim-light shaders.
- **V-HACK In-Memory Surgery**: Real-time texture replacement and submesh editing with GLB export.
